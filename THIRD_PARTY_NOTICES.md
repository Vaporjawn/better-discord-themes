The `vendor/` folder holds an unmodified copy of a larger BetterDiscord theme
collection with its own per-theme readmes. The `archive/` folder holds two
themes by DevilsLynAvenged written for Discord's 2018 class names.

original work under the MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Themes marked
"Unknown (collected by Vaporjawn)" have no identified author; they are listed
here so their provenance is clear and can be corrected.
